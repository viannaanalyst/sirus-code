# ADR-146: A team card for multi-agent turns, and a live view per subagent

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

Subagents already show as rows inside a reply (ADR-035, ADR-097, ADR-111, ADR-115): one card per child with a state pill, its time window and its newest 20 generic steps. A turn that fans work out to several subagents reads as a list scattered through the reply, and what a subagent actually said, thought and ran is not shown anywhere: the row keeps only step labels. Claude's stream already carries each child's messages (`parent_tool_use_id` on `assistant`/`user` frames), and Codex already routes a spawned agent's own thread items to its row, but both are reduced to step labels and the rest is dropped.

## Decision

- **Team card.** A reply whose turn started two subagents or more opens with a compact, collapsible card (`SubagentTeam.tsx`, pure logic in `lib/subagent-team.ts`): a headline (the first Markdown heading the agent wrote before its first launch, else the first line of the person's prompt), progress (done of total, running, failed), and one row per subagent with the provider or model mark, its label, a background mark, tools used (kept plus folded steps; a workflow's members' tool calls and settled/total), elapsed time from its native window, and its state (three hopping dots while running, like the header tabs; a glyph once done, failed, stopped or unreported). Subagents launched back to back — no other work between them, at the same place in the reply — form one phase; any other work, or text, starts the next; "finished" markers and background shells are not members. Phase titles show only when there are two phases or more. The card re-renders with its own turn's activity and ticks on its own one-second clock while a member runs.
- **Live view.** A row opens that subagent in the right dock as a `subagent` pane (bound to its conversation, at most six, not remembered when closed): a back button that closes it and returns focus to the composer, the mark, name, elapsed time, "Read-only" and state, then its feed in order — what it was asked (clamped, opens on click), its messages (Markdown), its thinking (first line, opens on click) and its tool calls (name, detail, state; output opens on click). It follows the newest entry while the reader is at the bottom. The newest 120 entries render (earlier ones in pages of 120) and rows use `content-visibility: auto`. With no feed, a finished subagent shows the steps its turn recorded.
- **Native capture (`subagent_feed.rs`).** One parser reads a Claude message line from the stream or from a transcript file (`claude_entries`: text, non-empty thinking and `tool_use` of `assistant` lines; the prompt, notes and `tool_result` of `user` lines; `isMeta`, compact summaries and attachments skipped); entries are keyed by line `uuid` and block index, tools by their call id, and results only settle a call already seen (never back to running). Codex child items give answers (`agentMessage`), reasoning summaries, the prompt (`userMessage`) and tools read like the parent's rows. Claude feeds are keyed by the launching call (`parent_tool_use_id`, the child row's `source`), Codex feeds by the child row (`agent:<thread>`). Every field is masked and scrubbed of privately provided values (ADR-077) and bounded: text 4,000 characters, output its last 2,000, detail one line of 240; 300 entries and 256 KiB per feed, 32 feeds per session, 12 sessions, least recent first. The turn loops emit `Event::Feed`, which the monitor handles before taking the data lock: it never touches the session, its persistence or `session-updated`.
- **Delivery.** `subagent-feed { sessionId, key, entries, dropped }` carries only what a frame added or changed, merged. `subagent_feed { sessionId, key }` returns the feed this run of the app saw (`live`), else, for Claude, the end (2 MiB) of the CLI's own transcript for that call — `<config>/projects/<cwd folder>/<native thread>/subagents/agent-<id>.jsonl`, found by the `.meta.json` whose `toolUseId` is the call, read only inside the Claude config folder's `projects` as a regular file (`transcript`) — else nothing (`none`). In the renderer, feeds live in a small external store (`store/subagent-feeds.ts`), kept only while a view watches them; events for unwatched feeds are dropped. A running subagent whose feed is not streaming (a background subagent, whose messages the CLI does not forward) is read again every 2 s while its view is shown and the window visible.
- Nothing new is written to `state.db`: a finished Claude subagent reopens from the provider's transcript; a finished Codex subagent reopens from memory while the app runs and from its recorded steps after a relaunch.

## Consequences

- Watching a subagent costs one bounded event per child frame and a re-render of its pane only; the transcript does not re-render for subagent work, and the team card only with its own turn.
- After a relaunch, Codex subagents show their recorded steps, not their messages (their rollouts are not parsed here). Claude workflow members (ADR-115) are one row of the card, showing members settled of all; their own answers stay in the existing workflow card.
- A single subagent keeps its existing card in the reply; the live view opens from the team card only.
